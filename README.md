# A1 Chicken Center - Web Platform

Fresh & Hygienic Chicken, Mutton & Poultry Products delivered directly to your doorstep.

## Overview
A modern web application for **A1 Chicken Center** featuring:
- Fresh chicken cuts (curry cut, boneless, drumsticks, wings, whole bird, biryani special)
- Mutton & specialty meats
- Farm fresh eggs & marinades
- Direct WhatsApp & online ordering
- Daily rates & special deals
- Location & delivery area check
- Order tracking & customer support

## Tech Stack & Setup
Built with modern web technologies for blazing fast performance and mobile-first responsiveness.
